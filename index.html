Our Philosophy

We don't just decorate spaces. We compose experiences.

At The Huntington Meadow, we believe that every celebration carries a story waiting to be told through design. Our approach begins with listening — to your vision, your heritage, your love story — and transforms those intimate details into environments that move people.

Founded on Long Island and rooted in a deep appreciation for the artistry of celebration, we bring together botanical elegance, architectural precision, and cultural sensitivity to create events that live forever in memory.

From grand weddings to intimate cultural ceremonies, every element is curated with intention. Because when the details are perfect, the moment becomes timeless.

Sustainability

Beauty without the footprint

Our luxury faux florals are indistinguishable from fresh — and every event that uses them is one less shipment on a cargo plane from South America, East Africa, or Europe. The global cut flower trade is a $35 billion industry — and 80% of the flowers sold in America are imported. We don't just create beautiful celebrations. We protect the world they happen in.

0 billion stems

Roses alone produced globally every year — flown on refrigerated cargo planes from Colombia, Ecuador, Kenya, Ethiopia, and the Netherlands to reach your table.

Fresh Cut Flowers

Lifespan: 7–12 days, then landfill
Transport: Refrigerated air freight from Colombia, Ecuador, Kenya, Ethiopia & the Netherlands
Water: ~3 gallons per single stem
Chemicals: Pesticide levels often exceed limits; many chemicals used are banned in the U.S.
Waste: 600 lbs. average waste per wedding

The Journey

From vision to reality

A seamless four-step process designed to bring your dream event to life.

Step 01

Discovery & Vision

We begin with an intimate consultation to understand your story, your aesthetic, your cultural traditions, and the atmosphere you want to create.

Step 02

Design & Concept

We create a bespoke design concept complete with mood boards, color palettes, layout plans, and curated decor selections tailored entirely to you.

Step 03

Curation & Sourcing

Every piece is hand-selected — from linens and florals to lighting and statement installations — ensuring cohesion and quality in every detail.

Step 04

Installation & Magic

On the day, our team transforms your venue with precision, artistry, and care — so you can simply arrive and be swept away by the beauty.
